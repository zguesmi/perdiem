# Prose Rules

Applies to every markdown file, plus issue text and commit bodies.

## Voice

Simplified Technical English, plus Zinsser. One idea per sentence, 20 words or fewer. Active voice,
present tense. Cut every word that does no work.

Use one word for one thing. Take the term from `CONTEXT.md` and keep it. Write short full names in
place of acronyms.

## Claims

Give the number: "Payout 440, refund 310", not "large savings". When you have no number, say so.

Mark anything unverified as unverified. Every statistic, citation and link must come from a source
you read.

## Shape

Lead with the answer. Put the context after it.

Use a list for a list and prose for reasoning. Keep a bullet to one sentence; longer thinking goes
in a paragraph.

Name each trade-off you took and what it cost. State a limitation as a limitation.

Write flat and factual. The reader is an engineer who has shipped things.
